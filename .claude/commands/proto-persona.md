# Proto-Persona Skill

## Context: Radar (Market Analyzer)
Radar is a Vite+React SPA used by Yektanet (Iranian ad-tech) sales managers to analyze advertiser CSV data — viewing spend trends, market share heatmaps, industry breakdowns, and AI chat insights.

## Proto-Persona: A Lightweight Approach to Early User Profiling

A proto-persona synthesizes existing research, market signals, and team knowledge into a working hypothesis about your target user before extensive validation occurs. It's designed to align teams quickly during early-stage product development without requiring months of research.

### Key Distinctions
Proto-personas differ from validated personas in timeline and confidence level. While proto-personas emerge within days using assumptions plus limited data, validated personas develop over weeks or months through rigorous user research.

### Primary Use Cases
Deploy proto-personas when:
- Beginning product development before comprehensive user research
- Launching new features or pivoting direction
- Unifying stakeholder perspectives on target audiences
- Mapping knowledge gaps that require future investigation

### Core Components
**Identity elements:** memorable names (preferably alliterative), demographics paired with behavioral context, and representative quotes revealing mindset and frustrations.

**Contextual understanding:** pain points, observable accomplishments the person pursues, and both short-term and aspirational goals.

**Influence mapping:** decision-making authority, who shapes their choices, and beliefs affecting product adoption.

### Critical Safeguards
Avoid treating proto-personas as validated fact. Mark uncertain assumptions with `[ASSUMPTION—VALIDATE]` tags.

## Your Task
Create 2-3 proto-personas for Radar's users. For each persona include:
1. Name + role at Yektanet (e.g., "Sales Manager Sara", "Account Manager Ali")
2. Demographics + behavioral context
3. A representative quote about their daily frustrations with data analysis
4. Key pain points with current tools
5. Short-term goals (daily/weekly) and aspirational goals
6. Who influences their decisions
7. Mark any unvalidated assumptions with `[ASSUMPTION—VALIDATE]`

Output the personas as a markdown document and save to `docs/personas.md`.
