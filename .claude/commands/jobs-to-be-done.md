# Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Skill

## Context: Radar (Market Analyzer)
Radar is a Vite+React SPA used by Yektanet sales managers to analyze advertiser CSV data — viewing spend trends, market share heatmaps, industry breakdowns, and AI chat insights.

## JTBD Framework

Discover what customers truly need by examining motivations across three dimensions: jobs, pains, and gains.

### Three Types of Jobs
- **Functional:** Concrete tasks — "Identify which advertisers reduced spend this week"
- **Social:** How customers want others to perceive them — "Appear data-driven and prepared in client calls"
- **Emotional:** Desired feelings — "Feel confident I'm not missing a churn signal"

### Pain Categories
- Challenges blocking progress
- Costly activities consuming time or money
- Common mistakes that could be prevented
- Unresolved problems current solutions don't address

### Gain Categories
- Expectations exceeding current solutions
- Time or money savings that delight customers
- Adoption factors driving switches from current tools
- Quality-of-life improvements

### Key Principle
Separate job from solution: "monitor advertiser health" (job) ≠ "CSV upload" (tool). This prevents anchoring on features over underlying needs.

### When to Use
Early discovery, product-market validation, roadmap prioritization, competitive analysis.

## Your Task
Identify the top 5 jobs-to-be-done for Yektanet sales managers using Radar. For each job:
1. State the functional, social, and emotional dimensions
2. List the top 2-3 pains currently blocking this job
3. List the top 2-3 gains a better solution would provide
4. Rate job importance (high/medium/low) and current satisfaction (high/medium/low)

Then identify 3 underserved jobs — high importance, low satisfaction — as prime product opportunities.

Save output to `docs/jobs-to-be-done.md`.
