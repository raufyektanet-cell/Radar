## Version 1: For Sales Managers (Daily Users)

### Value Proposition

**For** Yektanet sales managers who own a portfolio of 15–40 advertisers,
**that need** to identify at-risk accounts and prepare competitive talking points before client conversations — without spending 45 minutes every morning manually scanning spreadsheet data,
**Radar is** a market intelligence tool purpose-built for Iranian ad-tech sales workflows
**that** cuts daily data preparation from 45 minutes to under 10 minutes by surfacing Yektanet share, competitor sessions, and spend trends in one Persian-language screen.

### Differentiation Statement

**Unlike** exporting data to Excel and building pivot tables by hand,
**Radar** pre-calculates advertiser-level market share, competitor session breakdowns, and trend signals in real time from a single CSV upload — so sales managers spend their time acting on insights, not producing them.

### Evaluation

**Is the differentiation defensible?** Yes — the differentiation is structural (Radar knows the specific agency columns, Persian naming conventions, and Yektanet business logic that no generic tool can match out of the box). A competitor could build the same thing, but the switching cost and domain specificity make this durable in the near term.

**Claim needing data to substantiate:** "Cuts daily data preparation from 45 minutes to under 10 minutes." This is plausible based on the manual workflow it replaces, but requires time-tracking with 3–5 sales managers before and after adoption to confirm.

## Version 2: For Yektanet Leadership (Decision-Makers)

### Value Proposition

**For** Yektanet sales leadership responsible for advertiser retention and revenue growth across 16 sales teams,
**that need** visibility into competitive market dynamics across the entire advertiser portfolio — without depending on engineering to pull custom reports or waiting for end-of-month aggregations,
**Radar is** an internal sales intelligence platform
**that** gives every sales team self-service access to daily advertiser health data, Yektanet market share trends, and AI-generated briefings — reducing the time from data to decision from days to minutes.

### Differentiation Statement

**Unlike** generic BI tools (Tableau, Power BI) that require data engineering support and weeks of dashboard configuration,
**Radar** is pre-configured for Yektanet's exact data structure, speaks Persian, and requires zero engineering involvement for day-to-day use — enabling 16 sales teams to operate independently from a single CSV upload.

### Evaluation

**Is the differentiation defensible?** Mostly yes. The Persian-language UX, domain-specific data model (agency columns, Yektanet business rules), and zero-engineering-dependency are real structural advantages. Generic BI tools cannot replicate this without custom development work that would cost more and take longer.

**The "weeks of configuration" claim against Tableau/Power BI is defensible** if Yektanet has never successfully deployed a BI dashboard to all 16 teams. If they have, this argument weakens — validate by asking leadership whether previous BI tool rollouts succeeded.

**Claim needing data to substantiate:** "Reduces time from data to decision from days to minutes." Requires measuring the current reporting cycle (how long from data availability to a sales manager taking action) vs. the Radar-enabled cycle.

For day-to-day product decisions, use the **Sales Manager version** as the primary lens — it's closest to the actual daily use case and keeps the team anchored to the user who opens Radar every morning.

Use the **Leadership version** when socializing the roadmap or requesting resources — it frames Radar as strategic infrastructure rather than a convenience tool.
